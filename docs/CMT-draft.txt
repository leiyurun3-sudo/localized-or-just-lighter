Title
Localized, or Just Lighter? Attributing Background Preservation in Flow-Based Image Editing

Abstract
Spatial masks change both where an editor acts and the magnitude of its updates. We separate these effects in two FLUX.1-dev editors. For a correction toward the source, spatial weighting raises background LPIPS by 0.0362 at the default coefficient. Replaying either native magnitude profile reverses the difference to −0.0100. In SAM-Flow at seed 0, two spatially constant gains fitted per image recover 84.5% of the default background gain on 42 images. Across all 50 images, mean directional CLIP falls from 0.0841 to 0.0521. At default strength, the edit-to-background MAE ratio averages 4.00 for spatial and 2.11 for uniform allocation. Matching directional CLIP within each image over scales up to one retains 21.3% of the default gap on 40 images with overlap. Replay measures the contribution of placement at prescribed magnitudes. Response matching compares complete configurations.
