\paragraph{Sensitivity to the sign of directional CLIP.}
Of the 42 SAM sources with background pixels, 40 had positive-width overlap at four settings and 41 at five. The upper endpoint of the shared directional-CLIP interval was at or below zero for 7/40 and 5/41, respectively. We excluded those sources and integrated over the entire shared interval of each remaining source, using the first crossing in scale order. On the 33 four-setting sources, the mean matched $U-S$ background feature-map LPIPS gap was $0.0283$ $[0.0173,0.0412]$, or $26.7\%$ $[18.4\%,34.9\%]$ of their mean scale-1 gap ($0.1060$). On the 36 five-setting sources, it was $0.0424$ $[0.0112,0.0691]$, or $36.4\%$ $[8.1\%,62.3\%]$ of $0.1164$. A second calculation restricted each integral to positive directional CLIP and divided by the retained interval width. This shortened 15 of the 33 intervals and 17 of the 36. The matched gaps were then $0.0290$ $[0.0164,0.0440]$ and $0.0427$ $[-0.0018,0.0781]$, retaining $27.3\%$ $[17.8\%,36.9\%]$ and $36.7\%$ $[-1.4\%,70.2\%]$, respectively. All brackets are 95\% percentile intervals from 10,000 paired source resamples (seed 0), with each source weighted equally and both means recomputed in every draw.
