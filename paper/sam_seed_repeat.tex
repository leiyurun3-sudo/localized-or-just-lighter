\subsection{Repeating the bounded SAM comparisons at seed 1}\label{sec:sam-seed-repeat}
We reran all 42 seed-0 sources with nonempty background masks. The source and mask files matched the original inputs by SHA-256. At seed 1 we regenerated each scout and fitted both scalar gains to that image's new native spatial trajectory. The solver used the same 28 steps and the internal reference-noise seed remained 999. Each source contributed ten outputs across the following settings. We ran spatial and uniform allocation at scales 0.25, 0.50, 0.75 and 1.00. Two more outputs used per-image constants or uniform allocation replaying the spatial profile. The 1.25 extension, FlowEdit and the reciprocal replay at the uniform profile belong to the seed-0 study.

\noindent\textit{Default comparisons.} For all 420 new outputs we scored background feature-map LPIPS, directional CLIP and regional MAE with the seed-0 weights and masks. Rescoring all ten archived configurations of the first eligible source in ID order reproduced seed-0 LPIPS and directional CLIP to within $5\times10^{-7}$ and reproduced both MAEs exactly.

Table~\ref{tab:sam-repeat-default} shows the default scores. At seed 1, the constants recover 82.4\% [74.7\%, 89.4\%] of the default background gain and reduce directional CLIP relative to spatial by 0.0422 [0.0232, 0.0627]. At the spatial magnitude profile, the spatial-minus-uniform allocation contrast is $-0.0178$ [$-0.0246$, $-0.0117$], compared with $-0.0172$ at seed 0. All 42 new replays met the FP32 projection tolerance on both branches at all 26 active steps. Neither branch exceeded a blend factor of $1+10^{-6}$ in those replays.

\noindent\textit{Matched response.} We applied the same four-scale first-crossing rule separately at each seed. Each retained fraction divides the mean matched gap by the mean default gap on the same sources. Positive gaps (uniform minus spatial) favor spatial allocation.

The seed-1 matched gap averages 0.0341 [0.0210, 0.0489] (Table~\ref{tab:sam-repeat-mrc}). The retained fraction is 26.8\% [16.3\%, 40.3\%] on all supported images and 36.9\% [24.9\%, 50.6\%] after excluding the eight nonpositive intervals. On the 40 images supported at both seeds, the mean matched gaps are 0.0263 and 0.0345. The paired seed-1 minus seed-0 change is $+0.0082$ [$-0.0015$, 0.0204]. Spatial has the lower matched loss on 35 of these images at both seeds, uniform on two, and three change sign. Intervals use 10,000 source-bootstrap draws, keeping the two measurements on each shared source paired. They describe source variability conditional on the measured seeds. Scores, source intervals and the comparison script are in \path{analysis/sam_seed_repeat/}.

A partial seed-2 run ended after four configurations on 12 sources (48 outputs), before the strength sweep. At the spatial profile, mean spatial-minus-uniform background LPIPS on these same sources was $-0.0240$, $-0.0265$ and $-0.0251$ at seeds 0, 1 and 2, respectively.
